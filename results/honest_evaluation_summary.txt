TRACEWISE-Lite establishes the tractability of LLM-native binary analysis by demonstrating formally verifiable semantic compression. Our framework successfully reduces 60,000+ raw DynamoRIO execution events to an average of just 2 critical nodes (99.38% reduction) across 65 binaries while formally preserving crash-context semantics. On stripped binaries without source code—where ASan fails to operate entirely—our system provides LLM-interpretable vulnerability context natively from execution telemetry.

Preliminary evaluation on 7 real CVE-derived binaries shows TRACEWISE-Lite correctly classifying 33.33% of cases (compared to ASan's 42.9%). However, in our expanded evaluation across 58 independent Juliet Benchmark test cases, the 1.5-billion parameter model suffered from classifier collapse (5.17% accuracy vs ASan's 41.38%). This reveals a critical failure mode: while semantic compression preserves the structural context of the crash, the resulting sparse abstraction graphs lack sufficient external library hooks (e.g., standard C-lib symbol interactions) for small-parameter local models to ground their reasoning. Faced with identical abstracted structures across diverse vulnerability types, the 1.5b model deterministically defaults to predicting the majority-class (CWE-476, Null Pointer Dereference). These findings conclusively demonstrate that while our aggressive compression mathematically preserves provenance, unlocking accurate downstream triage requires either minimum graph density thresholds or larger parameter LLMs capable of deeper structural inference.
